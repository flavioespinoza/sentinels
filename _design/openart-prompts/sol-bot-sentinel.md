## Character Block

```txt
Sol Bot Sentinel. A watchtower guardian standing over an implied trading floor. Tall, wide-shouldered mech, military-grade polished gunmetal armor. The helmet is a wide trapezoidal visor design, much wider than the shoulders, giving a watchtower silhouette -- like a sentry box worn as a helmet. Across the full width of the visor runs a single horizontal amber glowing slit, the eye, saturated gold-amber color (#f59e0b), casting a warm amber reflection on the chest plate below. The chest plate has mounting points for insignia but is bare. The stance is upright and still, a guard that never moves unless something crosses a line. Language emblem: polished C-sharp insignia engraved on the right shoulder pauldron, clean military style. Accent glow: amber, eye only.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime, low detail
