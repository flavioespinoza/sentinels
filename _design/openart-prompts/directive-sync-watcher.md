## Character Block

```txt
Directive Sync Watcher. A rule enforcer. The helmet is angular and pointed -- trapezoidal with a sharp spike or fin rising from the top center, like a blade or a law-gavel crest. The overall head silhouette is a pointed arch or gothic spire. The face plate is recessed and dark, and the eye is a diamond shape, sharp four points, glowing violet (#8b5cf6), set in the center of the face. The body armor has clean lines, TypeScript-language circuit-board trace engraved on the left shoulder, tight panel seams suggesting precision. The stance is upright and watchful, weight balanced equally, suggesting a process that never deviates from its rules. The spike on the helmet is the character's signature. Accent glow: violet, eye only.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime
