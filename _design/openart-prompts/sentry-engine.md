## Character Block

```txt
Sentry Engine. The heaviest, most armored mech in the roster. Massive rectangular cube helmet, nearly twice the width of the shoulders, with exposed wiring bundles at the neck joint and bolt-pattern corners -- four large structural bolts, one at each corner of the helmet. Heat-sink ridges run across the top of the cube head. The body is broader and stockier than every other sentinel in the set -- this is the heaviest armor. A single centered eye, a steel-blue glowing orb (#3b82f6), sits in the exact middle of the helmet face, surrounded by a dark iris recess. The eye has a faint inner pupil. Extra-wide shoulder armor plates, angular, layered, suggesting tank-grade protection. Language emblem: exposed heat-sink fins and wiring on the left shoulder, suggesting C++ mechanical complexity. Accent glow: steel blue, eye only.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, thin build, narrow shoulders, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime, low detail
