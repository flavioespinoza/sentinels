## Character Block

```txt
Corpus Sentinel. A gate guardian. The helmet is an arch shape -- a Gothic or triumphal arch, the top of the helmet curving upward to a point like a doorway or a keystone. The arch shape is the character's most distinctive feature: it communicates gate, boundary, threshold. The inner face plate sits inside the arch frame, slightly recessed. A single circular violet-purple glowing eye (#a78bfa) sits centered in the lower half of the arch face, surrounded by a dark recess. The arch is the frame around a doorway and the eye is what stands behind it. The body armor is standard dark gunmetal with Rust-language engraving -- circuit-trace oxidation patterns on the shoulders. The stance is centered and blocking, both feet planted as if standing in a doorway that nothing passes without authorization. Accent glow: violet-purple, eye only.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime
