## Character Block

```txt
FX Watch. A filing and renaming sentinel. The face IS the lens: the entire face plate is replaced by a large camera lens, fully recessed into a rectangular helmet frame. The lens has four or five concentric metal rings -- outer bezel, then decreasing rings of increasing darkness toward the center, with a bright yellow aperture glow (#fbbf24) at the very core. Eight aperture-blade notches are visible around the outer ring, like a real camera iris. The body armor has a utilitarian matte finish, no insignia, no engravings -- the shell-script aesthetic: function over form, nothing extra. The lens on the face is the single statement. The stance is efficient, ready to capture, arms at sides, weight slightly forward. Accent glow: yellow, lens core only.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime
