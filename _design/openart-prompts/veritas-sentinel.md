## Character Block

```txt
Veritas Sentinel. A black-box recorder. The defining feature is the eye: a large circular camera lens set directly into the face plate of the helmet, much larger than any other sentinel's eye, taking up most of the helmet face. The lens has concentric rings -- an outer metal bezel, a middle recess ring, an inner iris, and a central glowing red pupil (#ef4444). The lens aperture lines are faintly visible like a camera iris. On each shoulder, a small red recording indicator light glows -- the only lights besides the main lens. The body armor shows wear: the JavaScript-generation aesthetic, slightly older panels, more battle-scarred than the newer Rust sentinels, paint worn at edges. The stance is watchful, slightly forward-leaning, as if recording everything. Accent glow: red, lens eye and recording shoulder lights.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime
