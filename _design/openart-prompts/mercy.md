## Character Block

```txt
Mercy. The medic of the roster, the oldest watcher. The helmet is rounded, with softer curves than the others -- not aggressive, but not weak either; a guardian, not a warrior. On the forehead of the helmet, a medic cross in raised relief, glowing soft green (#4ade80). The face plate has two eyes instead of one -- two small glowing green circles, side by side, giving a more humanoid or caring expression. The body armor is worn in the JavaScript-generation style, older panels, patched repairs visible at the joint seams, suggesting a long service record. Proportions slightly shorter and stockier than the taller sentinels, grounded. A small medical-cross emblem is repeated on the left chest plate. The stance is protective, arms slightly forward and open, as if standing between something vulnerable and something dangerous. Accent glow: soft green, forehead cross and two eyes.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime, aggressive pose, weapon
