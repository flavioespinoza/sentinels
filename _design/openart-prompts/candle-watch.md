## Character Block

```txt
Candle Watch. A mech guardian carrying a lantern. The helmet is a rounded rectangular shape with softened corners, slightly shorter than the other sentinels -- a watchman's helm rather than a warrior's. The eye is a warm oval glow, deep orange (#fb923c), set in the center of the rounded face. From the left shoulder a mechanical lantern hangs on a short chain -- a geometric lantern, angular glass panels around a glowing orange flame core, casting a warm cone of light downward onto the left arm. The lantern is the character's signature and the brightest element besides the eye. The rest of the body is standard armored mech in dark gunmetal. Rust-circuit engravings on the right arm suggest the Rust language -- subtle oxidation-red trace patterns. Accent glow: warm orange, eye and lantern.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime
