## Character Block

```txt
Obligation Reader. A mech that reads and compares. The helmet is a flat rectangular block, scholar-like, with thin horizontal scan lines etched across the face plate -- like pages of a ledger. The eye is a diamond-shaped jade glow (#34d399), centered on the face, four-pointed, sharp. On the chest plate, a mounted ledger panel: a dark recessed rectangle with horizontal ruled lines in faint jade, as if data is being read line by line. The right arm ends in a hand holding a stylized geometric book, open, with jade light spilling from its pages. Circuit-trace engravings on the panels suggest Rust -- subtle reddish oxidation patterns in the panel seams. The stance is analytical, weight shifted slightly back, as if reading. Accent glow: jade, eye and ledger panel.
```

**Negative prompt:** organic shapes, human face, hair, skin, plants, bright environment, white background, watermark, text, cartoon, anime
